# Which intelligent textbook fits which AP course

*Built 2026-10-09 from the 118 books on [the case-studies page](https://dmccreary.github.io/intelligent-textbooks/case-studies/)
and the official AP Course and Exam Descriptions in `public/ced/`. Chapter numbers are the book's own
(`/chapters/NN-…/`). All of these books are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: credit Dan McCreary (or the listed author),
link the license, and share adaptations under the same license (see `src/data/packs/*` `credits`).*

**How to read this.** Each AP unit lists the chapters that cover it. **Strong** means the chapter teaches
most of what the CED asks for that unit; **partial** means it covers some of it, or covers it at a
different level or from a different angle (for example, Python instead of Java). Units with no match say
**none**. A book being listed is a starting point, not a substitute for writing against the CED.

## What this means for study packs

The courses with **strong** coverage across most units, in order of AP enrollment, are the best next
candidates: AP Psychology (Units 3–5), AP US History, AP Biology (Units 3–8), AP Calculus AB
(Units 3–6), AP Statistics, AP US Government, AP Environmental Science, AP Chemistry, AP Physics 1,
AP Macroeconomics, AP Precalculus.
