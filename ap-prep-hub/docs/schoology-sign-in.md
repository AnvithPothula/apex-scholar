# Connecting Schoology to Apex Scholar

Apex Scholar is a free AP exam prep site with a study scheduler. Connecting Schoology is optional. It has two parts, and they do different jobs.

| Part | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar feed | Brings your assignments and due dates into the scheduler as tasks | Your Schoology iCal link |
| Schoology sign-in | Marks a task done once you've submitted that assignment | Signing in with your Schoology account |

## Set it up

1. Sign in to Apex Scholar at [apex-scholar.com](https://apex-scholar.com). Accounts are free.
2. Open **Settings**, then **Class calendar**.
3. **Calendar feed:** in Schoology, open Settings, find "Share Your Schoology Calendar", click Enable and copy the iCal link. Paste it under **Calendar URL** and click **Set URL**.
4. **Sign-in:** under **Schoology sign-in**, type your school's Schoology address (the one you use to open Schoology, such as `yourschool.schoology.com`) and click **Sign in with Schoology**.
5. Approve Apex Scholar on the Schoology page. You'll return to Settings, which shows "Connected as" your name.
6. Click **Sync Now** to bring in assignments and check for submitted work.

Syncing also runs automatically if you turn on **Automatic Sync**. Submitted work is checked at most every 10 minutes that way, and every time you click **Sync Now**.

## What Apex Scholar can read

Access is read-only. Apex Scholar reads your own sections, their assignments, and your own submissions for those assignments. It never posts, submits, edits or deletes anything in Schoology, and it can't read another person's submissions.

Your Schoology token is stored on the server in the United States and is never sent to your browser. Your data is not sold, shared or used for advertising. See the [privacy policy](https://apex-scholar.com/privacy).

## What to expect

- Only assignments with a Schoology dropbox show as submitted. Tests, discussions and paper work don't, so tick those tasks off yourself.
- A task is ticked at most once. If you untick it, it stays open.
- Schoology ends each sign-in after 90 days. Settings then shows **Sign in again**.
- Your district can turn off third-party apps. If sign-in is blocked, ask your school's Schoology administrator. The calendar feed still works without sign-in.

## Disconnecting

In **Settings**, then **Class calendar**, click **Sign out** under Schoology sign-in. This stops all access and deletes the stored token. To also remove the calendar feed, click **Disconnect**. You can also revoke Apex Scholar from your Schoology account's settings.
