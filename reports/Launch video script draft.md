## YouTube

Title: Apex Scholar: a free, open-source study app for every AP class

Description:
Apex Scholar is a free, open-source study app for AP students. Answer 10 questions to get a predicted score and a plan for today. Learn each unit with lessons and interactive sims, or ask an AI tutor, which can build you a simulation or a graph you can drag. Practice tests are laid out like the digital AP exam, and you can photograph your handwritten free-response work to have it graded against the rubric. Missed questions come back in a spaced review queue. There are also flashcards (with Quizlet import), a photo problem solver, a study scheduler that imports your Schoology or Canvas calendar, diagnostics, class leaderboards and AP score calculators. It covers all 37 AP courses. Built by a student, for students.

Try it: https://apex-scholar.com
