Title:
Apex Scholar - free AP exam prep I built as a student. 98 accounts, 5 active, and I'd like to know why

Body:
I'm a student and I built this while studying for my own AP exams, because I wanted everything in one place. It's free with no ads or paywall, and the code is open source.

You pick a subject, answer 10 questions without making an account, and get a predicted AP score, the range it could really be, and what to study today. Past that there are AI tutors, practice tests in the current exam format (free responses can be typed or photographed), flashcards and unit study packs.

The honest numbers: 98 accounts so far, 2 new in the last 30 days, and only 5 people signed in during that time. So almost nobody comes back. Knowt also gives away a lot of the same things, so "free and AI" isn't enough. My bet is on being straight about the score: College Board doesn't publish its cut points, so I show a range and say it's an estimate.

One thing I learned: the question bank is AI-generated, so I audited it. Re-solving and hand-checking turned up 14 wrong answer keys and 6 questions with no correct choice in one pass, and about 30 more fixes when two models re-solved the 1,082 quantitative questions.

What I'd like from you: try the 10 questions in any subject, even a guess-through, and tell me where you'd have quit and whether the result feels believable.
