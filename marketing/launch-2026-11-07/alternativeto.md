Description:
Apex Scholar is a free study site for AP exams, covering 37 subjects. Answer 10 questions with no account and get a predicted AP score, the range it could really be, and what to study today. It also has AI tutors, practice tests in the current exam format with free-response answers that can be typed or photographed, flashcards, unit study packs, score calculators and a study scheduler. No ads and no paywall. It's built by a student and the code is open source. Score predictions are estimates, because College Board doesn't publish its cut points. Not affiliated with College Board.
